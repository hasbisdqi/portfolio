# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters, engineering managers, and peer developers evaluating technical skills and depth during the hiring process.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio and engineering blog that serves as a living technical resume. Success means effectively demonstrating technical competence and engineering rigor to prospective employers.

## Positioning
Focused on deep, substantial technical writeups that showcase rigorous understanding and problem-solving, rather than just surface-level project galleries.

## Operating Context
Viewed during the hiring and interview process. Often scanned quickly by recruiters initially, then read deeply by engineering interviewers evaluating the candidate's technical bar.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Built as a static/hybrid web application using Next.js App Router, Tailwind CSS, and Notion for content management.

## Brand Commitments
- Name/Moniker: "rnghbt"
- Profile Identity: Uses "Doofus Rick" (from Rick and Morty) as the avatar/profile picture.

## Evidence on Hand
- Technical blog content sourced via Notion (inferred from package dependencies).

## Product Principles
- **Depth over breadth**: Favor comprehensive technical explanations over lists of shallow features.
- **Unpretentious competence**: Let the technical rigor speak for itself, anchored by a relaxed personal brand (rnghbt / Doofus Rick).
- **Frictionless reading**: Ensure the reading experience for long-form technical content is fast, accessible, and distraction-free.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web accessibility guidelines (WCAG) to ensure recruiters and developers using assistive technologies can navigate and read the writeups without barriers.
